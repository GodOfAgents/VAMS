# VAMS Research

Use this skill to convert research into executable engineering decisions.

## Workflow

1. Identify the paper, claim, or proposed mechanism.
2. If external or current, verify the source before relying on it.
3. Read relevant VAMS docs and code paths.
4. Use `references/research-map.md` to map concepts to subsystems.
5. Separate implemented, partial, stubbed, and aspirational pieces.
6. Express formulas in LaTeX and identify parameters.
7. Define tests, metrics, and failure modes before recommending implementation.

## Output Shape

- **Research Claim:** precise mechanism and source.
- **VAMS Mapping:** files, modules, and current maturity.
- **Math/Protocol Model:** equations, variables, thresholds.
- **Implementation Plan:** narrow steps and tests.
- **Risks:** security, economic, cognitive, deployment, and mock-mode risks.
- **Decision:** adopt, prototype, reject, or defer.
